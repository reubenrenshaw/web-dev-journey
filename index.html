Mandarin Text to Anki Flashcard Deck

This application automates the creation of Anki flashcard decks using CEDICT and the Deepl translation API. It is compatible with both simplified and traditional characters and employs multithreading with large texts. Texts are inputed and the application creates Anki flashcard decks with all of the unique words contained within the chosen text.
